Carob Health privacy policy

Version 2026-11-09. Effective 9 November 2026.

The short version

Carob Health keeps your medical data, documents and entries on your phone. There is no account and no server for them. We — Antere Software Ltd — never receive them, cannot see them, and cannot recover them. The only way they leave your device is when you export or share them yourself.

The app sends us one kind of thing: technical data — crash reports and anonymised usage statistics — so that we can keep it working and make it better. That data contains nothing you entered, and you can turn it off at any time. The details are in Crash reports and usage statistics.

The rest of this policy explains that in the detail the law and the app stores require.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the information you choose to enter. All of it lives in a database on your device, encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher); document files are encrypted separately with their own key. Both keys are generated on your device and kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, encrypted storage on Android). Depending on what you use, this includes:

  • Your profile: a name if you give one, sex at birth, year of birth, height and weight, blood group, skin and hair colour for your figure, allergies, and health conditions or physical particulars you record.
  • Medications and courses, with schedules, doses and stock counts.
  • Doctor visits and their outcomes, and a contact list of doctors and clinics you add.
  • Measurements such as blood pressure, blood sugar and weight.
  • Your health journal.
  • Documents you photograph or import — images and PDFs — and the text recognised from them.
  • Reminder settings, and a verifier for your PIN (not the PIN itself).

Much of this is health data, which the GDPR treats as a special category. Because it is stored and processed only on your device under your control, we do not process it, and no one at Antere has access to it. The app does not back it up to iCloud or Google's cloud backup: it is excluded from system backups on both platforms.

What leaves your device

Your medical data, documents and entries: nothing, unless you send them. Specifically:

  • Backups. You can export an encrypted backup file (.mhb), protected by a passphrase you choose. You decide where to save or send it. We cannot open it and cannot recover the passphrase.
  • Reports and the allergy card. You can create a PDF report for a doctor or share your allergy card. These go wherever you send them. The allergy card contains only your allergies and their severity — no name and no other identifying details.
  • Links and phone numbers. Tapping a doctor's phone number or address opens your phone or maps app. The app passes only that number or address.

The app contains no advertising. It may send us crash reports and anonymised usage statistics — technical data that contains nothing you entered. What they include, who processes them and how to turn them off is described in the next section.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Carob Health keeps all your medical data, documents and entries on your device only and never transfers them. To make the app more stable and better, from version 0.12.0 it may send us two categories of technical data. Both are on by default, and both can be turned off at any time: in Settings → Privacy and data, and on the screen shown at first launch.

Crash reports. If the app terminates with an error, we receive: the error type and stack trace (file and function names of the program), the app version and build, the OS version and device model, language and time zone, a list of recent screen transitions (screen names without content), and technical session details (launch, background, exit) used to calculate the crash-free session rate. Reports never include your entries, names of medications, doctors or clinics, measurement values, documents, your name or profile, your IP address (not stored), or any user or device identifier. Error messages are automatically reduced to the error type before sending. Processor: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA); data is received and stored on servers in the European Union (Germany) for 90 days; a data processing agreement is in place. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); you may object by turning crash reports off.

Usage statistics. We receive anonymised events about which features are used: the event name and its categories (for example, "event completed — medication — from feed"), the app version, operating system, language, country (derived from the network address on receipt; the address itself is not stored), the week the app was installed and the number of days since installation. Counts are transferred as ranges ("2–5", "6–20"). The app uses no advertising or persistent device identifiers: a technical identifier changes every calendar day, so events from different days cannot be linked to one person. Statistics never include anything you entered. Processor: Amplitude, Inc. (USA), servers in the European Union; a data processing agreement is in place. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the product (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); when you turn statistics off, one final "statistics disabled" event is sent, after which transmission stops.

Network. Apart from the two categories above, the app does not use the network to process your data: text recognition, encryption, backups and doctor reports run on the device.

The two processors, for the record:

  • Sentry — Functional Software, Inc., San Francisco, USA. Receives the crash reports described above. Data is received and stored in the European Union (Germany, EU data residency) and deleted after 90 days. A data processing agreement, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, is part of Sentry's terms.
  • Amplitude — Amplitude, Inc., San Francisco, USA. Receives the usage statistics events described above. Data is stored in the European Union (EU data centre) and kept for the standard retention period of our plan; what we work with are aggregates. A data processing agreement is in place.

Both companies are based in the United States; the data itself is stored in the European Union, and each has signed a data processing agreement with us.

On-device processing

Text recognition (OCR) for pharmacy stickers and documents, and barcode scanning, run entirely on your device. The images you scan are not uploaded anywhere. On iOS the app uses Apple's built-in Vision framework; on Android it uses Google's ML Kit together with the open-source Tesseract engine, with the recognition models bundled inside the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to photograph documents and scan pharmacy stickers and barcodes.
  • Photos and files — to import existing images and PDFs, and to save backups where you choose.
  • Notifications and exact alarms — for reminders at the time you set, including after the phone restarts.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint — to unlock the app without typing the PIN. The biometric check is done by the operating system; the app never sees your biometric data.

Each permission is optional. Declining it turns off the related feature and nothing else.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes the database, all document files, both encryption keys, your settings and all scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. Backup files and reports you saved or sent elsewhere are outside the app, and deleting them is up to you.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. For Carob Health, you exercise all of them directly in the app: everything it holds is visible on screen, editable, exportable as a backup, and deletable. We hold no copy of your medical data and cannot identify you from it, so there is nothing further for us to provide or delete — but if you have a question or believe something is wrong, write to support@anteresoftware.com. If you email us, we process your message and address to answer you, as described in our website privacy policy.

The technical data described in Crash reports and usage statistics is different in one respect. The app has no accounts and no user identifiers, so we cannot tell which crash reports or statistics came from you: we are unable to link that data to a particular person, and therefore unable to find and delete "your" records on request. The way to stop the processing is to turn off the corresponding switch in the app (Settings → Privacy and data); from then on, nothing is sent. If you have questions about this, write to support@anteresoftware.com.

You can complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or to the supervisory authority in your own country.

Children

Carob Health is intended for adults managing their own health. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The app has no accounts, and the technical data it sends carries no persistent identifiers and cannot be linked to a person, so we have no way of knowing who its users are.
