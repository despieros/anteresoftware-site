Independent mobile software, made in Limassol.

Antere Software designs and builds applications for iPhone and Android. A small team, products meant to last, and no data we don't need.

What we do

We take a product from an idea to the App Store and Google Play: product design, native development for iOS and Android, release, and the long tail of maintenance afterwards.

Our applications are built to ask for only the data they need to work. Each app has its own privacy policy and terms, published on this site and linked from its store listing.

If you use one of our apps and something is wrong, you can reach a person. Questions, feedback, and support requests all go to support@anteresoftware.com.
